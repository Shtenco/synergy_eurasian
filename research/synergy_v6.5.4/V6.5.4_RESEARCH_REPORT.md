# SYNERGY V6.5.4 — Network Crisis Resilience Kernel

## Executive result

This is a synthetic research prototype, not a calibrated forecast or a production credit/risk system.

The model represents **80 economic nodes in four sectors**, simulated over 100 days. It compares four policies under moderate, severe and systemic persistent shocks.

Executed research:
- Main grid: 100 paired seeds × 3 regimes × 4 policies = **1,200 policy paths**.
- Systemic precision: 300 paired seeds × 4 policies = **1,200 policy paths**.
- Capital sensitivity: 7 capital levels × 100 seeds = **700 policy paths**.
- Total: **3,100 executed policy paths**.

## Policies

1. `isolated`: no shared liquidity pool and no supplier substitution.
2. `liquidity_pool`: prefunded 100-unit liquidity pool.
3. `firewall`: supplier substitution after a supplier defaults; new supplier choice affects future orders only.
4. `adaptive_synergy`: supplier substitution plus a prefunded 100-unit liquidity pool and 100-unit reserve.

No token price, market capitalization or unbacked credit is counted as capital. The pool and reserve are explicit synthetic cash commitments.

## Main experiment

| Regime | Policy | Mean defaults | 95th percentile defaults | Mean survival |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| moderate | isolated | 0.00 | 0.00 | 1.000 |\n| moderate | liquidity_pool | 0.00 | 0.00 | 1.000 |\n| moderate | firewall | 0.00 | 0.00 | 1.000 |\n| moderate | adaptive_synergy | 0.00 | 0.00 | 1.000 |\n| severe | isolated | 1.05 | 6.25 | 0.987 |\n| severe | liquidity_pool | 0.99 | 6.25 | 0.988 |\n| severe | firewall | 1.05 | 6.25 | 0.987 |\n| severe | adaptive_synergy | 0.92 | 0.55 | 0.988 |\n| systemic | isolated | 7.02 | 30.10 | 0.912 |\n| systemic | liquidity_pool | 6.28 | 30.05 | 0.922 |\n| systemic | firewall | 6.80 | 30.00 | 0.915 |\n| systemic | adaptive_synergy | 5.10 | 29.05 | 0.936 |\n
Moderate regime: no defaults in the 100-seed sample for any policy.
Severe regime: adaptive policy averages 0.92 defaults versus 1.05 for isolated.
Systemic regime: adaptive policy averages 5.10 defaults versus 7.02 for isolated in the 100-seed main grid.

The main-grid results are directional. The severe-regime difference is small and its paired confidence interval includes zero. The systemic regime shows the clearer benefit.

## Systemic precision test: 300 paired seeds

| Policy | Mean defaults | 95% CI for mean defaults | Collapse frequency |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| isolated | 7.74 | 5.83–9.65 | 3.00% |\n| liquidity_pool | 6.96 | 5.24–8.69 | 3.00% |\n| firewall | 7.53 | 5.61–9.46 | 3.00% |\n| adaptive_synergy | 5.82 | 4.16–7.48 | 1.33% |\n
Paired reduction in defaults versus isolated:
- Liquidity pool: **0.78 fewer defaults**, 95% CI 0.59–0.96.
- Supplier rerouting: **0.21 fewer defaults**, 95% CI 0.13–0.28.
- Adaptive Synergy: **1.92 fewer defaults**, 95% CI 1.58–2.26.

Collapse is defined here as 40 or more defaults out of 80 nodes. In the 300-seed systemic sample, isolated had a 3.0% collapse frequency and adaptive Synergy 1.33%. This is a sample frequency under a deliberately stylized stress model, not a real-world probability forecast.

More prefunded capital improves survival in this sample, but it is not free. The model does not charge the pool for funding cost, opportunity cost, governance overhead, or losses from misallocated rescue. The capital curve must not be interpreted as an optimal reserve recommendation.

## Core model assumptions

- 80 nodes, 4 sectors, 20 nodes per sector, 100 days.
- Starting cash: 30 per node.
- Revenue: 10 per production unit.
- Variable cost: 6 per production unit.
- Fixed cost: 2 per live node per day.
- Debt service: 0.5 per live node per day.
- Two supplier links per node.
- 35% of each node's sales is scheduled as an intercompany supplier payment, split between its two suppliers and due after three days.
- Invoices retain their original supplier destination. Supplier rerouting changes only future purchases.
- Production is reduced when suppliers default.
- Persistent macro and sector shocks differ by regime.
- Nodes default when their cash becomes negative after scheduled settlements, operating costs and any available prefunded support.
- Consolidated operating surplus counts modeled external sales less variable costs, fixed costs and debt service. Intercompany transfers cancel at network level.

## What the model does not prove

It does not prove that Synergy prevents real bankruptcies, that the network is economically optimal, or that the reserve is sufficient. It excludes taxes, payroll, households, inventory, collateral, legal workout, bank funding, price changes, capacity investment, endogenous demand substitution and real contract data.

The current supplier firewall is a simple heuristic, not a complete network optimization algorithm. The Gaussian persistent shocks are stylized. Real-world validation requires calibrated company-level data, counterparty links, payment histories and independently reviewed stress assumptions.
