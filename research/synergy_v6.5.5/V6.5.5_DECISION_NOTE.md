# V6.5.5 Decision Note

**Decision:** retain the self-healing concept, but do not declare the current network optimizer the winning policy.

The optimizer wins on production lost, external gross sales, operating surplus, and recovery time in the systemic 500-seed experiment. Liquidity-first wins on mean defaults and final recovery ratio. The optimizer's ex-post CVaR95 of defaults is lower, but that does not establish a reliable tail-risk advantage.

**Next engineering decision:** build a joint allocator that evaluates repair and liquidity actions in one scarce-capital budget. The objective must expose explicit weights or constraints for:
1. expected external value preserved;
2. probability of node survival;
3. CVaR95 of network loss;
4. intervention cost and remaining liquidity;
5. time to restore 90% of pre-shock output.

Do not use token prices, internal transfer volume, or mark-to-market asset values as external value. Only external sales and realized external cash flows count.
