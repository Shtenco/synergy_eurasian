# V6.5.6 Decision Note

Do not select a winner based on one metric. Compare the four policies across:
- mean defaults and collapse frequency;
- external gross sales and operating surplus;
- net result after interventions;
- recovery time and recovery ratio;
- reserve consumed and left unused.

The `cvar_robust` label describes a conservative heuristic only. It is not a formal CVaR-constrained optimum. The next major upgrade should estimate each intervention's incremental value through paired counterfactual simulation and then solve a constrained allocation problem.
